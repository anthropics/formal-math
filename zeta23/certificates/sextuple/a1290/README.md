# `A = 129/10000 = 0.0129` sextuple certificate: provenance, catalog v2/v3, frontier analysis

Supporting material for the refined unconditional sextuple improvement
`Zeta23.ThmD.Sextuple.A1290.thmD₀_sextuple` (`0.67280056…`, public fixed constant `6728005676/10^10`) and its
kernel-checked five-dimensional certificate `Zeta23.ThmD.Sextuple.A1290.Certificate.sextuple_affine`

## What is new relative to `A1285`

`A = 0.0129` does **not** close over the 272-model catalog (2026-08-27: 6,000,000-node cap, ~40 boxes pending
forever). The reason is a catalog defect, not the kernel: at the tight point
`p* ≈ (6.605, 12.506, 6.566, 12.506, 6.605)` the five-gap span `d ≈ 44.787` sits on the seam between refined well
piece 42 and the 4.8-wide constant **barrier** piece 43 (`a = 0.000237`), while the true `2K(d)²` there is
`0.000368`; the surrogate parks the span just past the seam and loses `0.000131` of the `0.000163` total gap.

**Catalog v2/v3** (`catalog/generate_refinement_catalog_v2.py`, Lean module `A1290/RefinementData2.lean`,
666-entry table `A1290/Catalog.lean : improvedCatalog2`):

* the 216 narrow wells of v1 (unchanged, indices 56..271);
* 24 narrow-well cells on the previously unrefined well piece 36 (38.138..38.513);
* 306 constant-barrier cells of width 1/64 over barrier piece 43 (44.787..49.564), each with
  `a = (floor₁₀⁻¹⁵ of Lean's own `kernelRange` lower bound for 2K² on the cell) − 10⁻¹⁵`, computed by the exact
  `Fraction` replica `catalog/kernel_interval.py` of `RatInterval`/`TrigInterval`/`KernelInterval` (the replica
  reproduces the committed stable barrier 43's `a` bit-for-bit; Lean accepts each cell in ≈ 0.4 s);
* (v3 only) 64 such cells over `[59, 60]`, beyond the stable domain, so that the root-box limit can be raised to 60.

All 394 v2/v3 pieces are Lean-checked (`refinement2Table_check`, 88 s). With v3 the catalog surrogate's ceiling at
`B₆` rises from `A* ≤ 0.0129037` to `A* ≤ 0.0129616`, against the **true-kernel ceiling `A* ≤ 0.0129938`**
(`catalog/true_kernel_ceiling.py`: minimising `Σ 2K(d)² + B·S` directly — the ceiling of the whole affine-certificate
method with this kernel, for any catalog: `R ≤ 0.6728111`). Moving `B` is dead: `dA*/dB = S(p*) ≈ 44.8` against the
neutral trade `≈ 46.7` (`catalog/map_ceiling_vs_B.py`, `ceiling-vs-B.json`).

The exact tree here was produced with catalog v2 and root-box limit 59 (`A ≤ 59·B₆ = 0.0129207`, the tail
condition of `affineTree_global_at`); its term codes (< 602) mean the same pieces in the 666-entry v3 catalog used
by the Lean modules.

  `post-anthropic-rh-artifacts/checkers/sextuple-improvement/frontier-2026-08-28/v3-refined-A-0p01293/`.

  integrated. Beyond it the remaining headroom to the method's ceiling is ≈ 6×10⁻⁶.
